New Venture Fit Analysis

A reference map: understand investor fit, then preserve the assessment.

Snapshot: October 6, 2026. Current app matches saved source version 8. Original interactive checkpoint: 3956aa4; current reconciliation follows it. This reference route does not claim a completed learner walkthrough.

One assessment through the app

1. Confirm answers

app.py → confirm
Checks confirmation and passes the structured profile to evaluation.

2. Explain fit

evaluation.py → evaluate
Compares founder values with reviewed criteria and returns reasons and a label.

3. Save and reopen

app.py → save_current_assessment / reopen_assessment
Saves a snapshot and restores it without recomputing.

Business cause and effect

Demo Seed Partners requires all founders to be full-time. Changing that answer to No makes a synthetic hard requirement fail and produces Poor Fit. Unknown information remains unknown.

Inspect the decision

Find all_founders_full_time in matching_rules.json. Then find _matches and hard_mismatch in evaluation.py. Known hard mismatches take precedence over missing essential evidence, remaining conditions, and Strong Fit.

Software cause and effect

Evaluating changed answers saves a new assessment. Earlier answers, rules, evidence, and results remain in SQLite. Reopening restores the selected snapshot without rerunning the evaluator.

Inspect preserved history

Find summary_json in app.py → save_current_assessment and reopen_assessment. In schema.sql, inspect assessments and assessment_results. The check is test_workflow.py → test_changed_input_appends_and_original_reopens_without_recalculation.

To change this, start here

  • Form and confirmation: app.py → form / confirm.
  • Fit reasoning: evaluation.py → evaluate. Review evidence before changing matching_rules.json.
  • Email saving: app.py → remember_email / assessment_drafts.
  • Response tracking: app.py → record_outreach / show_outreach. Separate events never change fit.
  • Downloads: app.py → funding_brief_pdf / shortlist_csv.

A practice to reuse

Describe an observable effect before changing code: changing full-time commitment should affect the relevant criterion while the original saved assessment stays unchanged. Check both the new result and the preserved history. The PRD's Cause-and-Effect Demonstration and the changed-input integration test connect that intention to evidence.
