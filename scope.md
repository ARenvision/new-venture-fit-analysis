# New Venture Fit Analysis

A local tool that turns a U.S. AI venture profile into an evidence-backed Funding Fit Brief.

## The Unique Kernel
Explain why a funding organization fits, where the fit breaks down, what is unknown, and what the founder should do next. Separate fit from evidence quality and funding probability.

## Who It Is For
U.S. AI founders from idea stage through early paying customers seeking angel or venture-capital funding. No incorporation or revenue prerequisite imposed by the app; investor requirements differ.

## The Core Loop
Complete the accepted 12-question intake; correct the venture summary; evaluate a researched investor pool; inspect the shortlist, contacts and email template; save and reopen the assessment or download it.

## Inspiration and Identity
Working name supplied by the user. Familiar Streamlit workflow with compact summaries and actionable results. Visual details remain open; do not repeat the accepted content interview.

## Why This Matters
Help founders focus funding research and prepare informed approaches. The user wants a competitive, useful entry, efficient progress and work worth being proud of, without stretching the work to fill the available time.

## What Working Looks Like
A synthetic founder receives supported investor assessments, changes a meaningful input and sees the reason for a changed result, then saves and reopens the original assessment intact.

## The POC Boundary
Local Streamlit app; SQLite storage; initial pool about 50 funding organizations including VC firms and angel groups. Per venture, target 30–50 relevant organizations screened and up to 5–10 suitable prospects, never padded. Report exact coverage when smaller. Six factors: stage, sector, geography, funding amount, traction and restrictions. Verified contacts, official approach routes, founder-reviewed email templates and simple manual outreach history. PDF brief and spreadsheet shortlist.

## Later
Public hosting, private accounts, comparison views and continuous research updates. Live research on each request remains unapproved; initial implementation proposal is pre-researched profiles with dates and sources.

## Explicitly Cut
Automatic outreach, personal phone numbers, guessed emails, LinkedIn sourcing, funding promises, success-probability scores and exhaustive U.S. coverage claims. Grants and accelerators are intake preferences only; their matching is outside this version. Existing ARenvision applications and databases remain outside this project.
